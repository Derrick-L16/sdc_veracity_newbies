# AI-use declaration — review before submitting

This template discloses material AI assistance. It is not a statement that the organisers have approved the work. The attached planning notes do not contain the complete official AI eligibility policy. Check the official event rules and ask organisers if they are unclear. No declaration can guarantee that a submission will not be disqualified.

## Suggested truthful declaration

> We used AI-assisted development tools, including ChatGPT, to help interpret our requirements, plan the workflow, generate and revise backend code, connect the existing frontend to APIs, draft automated tests, and prepare technical documentation. The supplied frontend was identified by our team as an AI-assisted prototype; we will identify its actual generator and any other tools used in our final tool log. Our team is responsible for reviewing, understanding, testing, modifying and explaining the submitted implementation. Veracity's runtime reconciliation uses deterministic rules and a local SQLite database; it does not use a generative AI model to decide or approve stock corrections. Human manager approval or an explicit, recorded override is required for consequential changes. Example datasets, where used, are synthetic and are identified as such. CSV imports are implemented; live Orbyte integration is proposed and is not claimed as completed.

Use the paragraph about team review only **after your team actually performs that work**. Do not say AI was used only for grammar, research or brainstorming: it generated substantial implementation in this update.
